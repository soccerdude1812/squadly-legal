Squadly Coach

Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026  ·  Published by Parle LLC

Squadly is a free app that helps youth-soccer coaches track playing time and manage lineups. Here's the whole privacy story, in plain words.

What Squadly stores

About you: your name, email, and an account ID — so you can sign in and your teams stay yours. Phone number only if you add one.

About your team: whatever you type in — player names, jersey numbers, playing time, attendance, lineups, and game history, plus photos or parent contact info if you choose to add them, and the email address of any co-coach you invite.

That's it. No location, no contacts, no browsing history, no usage analytics.

What we never do

We don't run ads, we don't use tracking or analytics SDKs, we don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing, and nothing you enter is used to train AI. Squadly's only use of your data is showing it back to you and your co-coaches.

Where it lives and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase (a hosted database service) on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit. Only the coaches on your team can see your team's data — and that's enforced by database-level security rules, not just by this promise. There are no public pages and no shareable links to player data.

About players (usually kids)

Players never use Squadly — no logins, no screens for them. You, the coach, decide what to enter about your players, and it's on you to have whatever consent your league or parents require (especially for photos). We do nothing with roster data except show it to your team's coaches. Parents: to see or remove your child's info, ask the coach — or email us at eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com and we'll help.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account, right in the app. It's immediate and permanent: teams you head-coach are erased entirely — rosters, games, everything (the app warns you first if co-coaches would lose access) — and teams you assist on simply lose you as a member. Want to remove just one player instead? Do that from the Roster tab. If deletion ever fails, email us and we'll do it by hand.

Changes and contact

If anything above materially changes, the date up top changes, and significant changes get flagged inside the app. Questions or requests: eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com — it goes straight to the developer.
