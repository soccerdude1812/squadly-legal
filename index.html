Squadly Coach

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026  ·  Published by Parle LLC

Squadly is a free app for youth-soccer coaches for tracking playing time and managing lineups.

What Squadly stores

About you: your name, email, and an account ID. A phone number only if you add one.

About your team: what you enter — player names, jersey numbers, playing time, attendance, lineups, and game history; photos and parent contact info if you choose to add them; and the email address of any co-coach you invite.

Squadly does not collect location, contacts, browsing history, or usage analytics.

What we never do

No ads, no tracking or analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing data for marketing, and nothing you enter is used to train AI. Your data is used only to show it back to you and your co-coaches.

Where it lives and who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (a hosted database service) on servers in the United States and encrypted in transit. Only the coaches on your team can see your team's data — enforced by database-level security rules. There are no public pages or shareable links to player data.

About players

Players never use Squadly — no logins, no screens for them. The coach decides what to enter and is responsible for any consent their league or parents require, especially for photos. Roster data is shown only to the team's coaches. Parents: to see or remove your child's info, ask the coach, or email eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account and TeamSnap connection are erased. A team you head-coach alone is erased entirely — roster, games, everything. A team with other coaches passes to the coach who joined it first, with its data, and the app shows you who before you confirm. Teams you assist on remove you as a member. To remove a single player, use the Roster tab. If deletion fails, email eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

Material changes update the effective date above; significant changes are flagged in the app. Questions or requests: eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com.
