Squadly Coach

Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026  ·  Published by Parle LLC

Squadly is a free iOS and watchOS app for youth-soccer coaches — it helps you build lineups, run a live game clock, and track every player's playing time. This policy explains what Squadly collects, why, and how you can control or delete it. It's written in plain English on purpose: you shouldn't need a lawyer to read your own app's privacy policy.

If you have a question this page doesn't answer, email eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com.

1. Who Squadly is for

Squadly's users are adult coaches. Players on a roster are not Squadly users — they don't sign in, and Squadly has no interface for them. Roster entries (a player's name, jersey number, playing time, etc.) are content the coach enters and manages, the same way a paper clipboard or spreadsheet would hold that information. Because youth-soccer rosters are typically made up of minors, we've written a dedicated section on that below (§6) — please read it if you coach kids under 13.

2. What we collect

About you, the coach:

  • Your name and email address (used to create and sign in to your account)
  • An optional phone number, if you provide one
  • A unique account ID generated when you sign up

About your team, which you enter:

  • Player names and, optionally, jersey numbers, positions, and photos
  • Attendance, availability, and playing-time records
  • Lineups, formations, sub plans, and game history (scores, events, notes)
  • Optional parent/guardian contact details (phone or email), if you choose to record them
  • Email addresses of any assistant coaches you invite to your team

About your team's coaches, from invites:

  • The email address you enter to invite someone shows up in that person's invite record so they can accept it and join your team.

We don't collect location, health, browsing history, financial information, or your device's contacts. We have no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs running in Squadly, so we don't collect general "usage data" about how you use the app either.

3. How we use it

Everything we collect is used for one purpose: making Squadly work.

  • Your account info lets you sign in and keeps your teams tied to you.
  • Roster, lineup, and game data is what powers the live game clock, substitution planner, and playing-time summaries — the core of the app.
  • Invite emails let a co-coach join your team.
  • Nothing you enter is used to train AI/ML models, build advertising profiles, or generate analytics for us to study your usage.

We do not advertise, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Squadly has no ad network, no third-party tracking SDKs, and nothing in the app phones home to a data broker. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first and tell you about it (see §8).

4. Where your data lives

Squadly's backend runs on Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database and authentication service, with servers located in the United States. Supabase is a data processor for us — they store the data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes. All data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Apple, as the App Store distributor, and Google, only if you use "Sign in with Google" once that option is available, may process account-related information as part of getting the app to your device or verifying your sign-in. Neither receives your roster data.

If you later choose to connect a TeamSnap account from Settings, Squadly would import your TeamSnap roster and schedule on your behalf — that feature is not active in this version of the app, and no TeamSnap data is accessed unless you explicitly connect it yourself.

5. Who can see it

Your team's data is visible only to coaches on that team — you, and any assistant coaches you've invited and who have accepted. This isn't just a promise in this document: it's enforced at the database level. Every table that holds your roster, games, or lineups has row-level security rules that check the signed-in user's identity before returning any row, so one coach's team data is never visible to another coach who isn't a member of that team. There's no public roster page, no shareable link that exposes player data, and no way for someone outside your team to look up your players.

6. Children's data

Squadly is built for adult coaches, not for children. It is not directed at kids, has no sign-in or interface for players, and doesn't knowingly collect any information directly from a child.

That said, most players on a youth-soccer roster are minors, and coaches use Squadly to track their names, jersey numbers, playing time, and — if the coach chooses to add them — photos. In that relationship, the coach is the one who decides what to enter and is responsible for having whatever consent their league or the players' parents require — especially before adding a player's photo. Squadly acts as the coach's tool, not as a separate party collecting data from the child.

We do not use roster data for anything beyond displaying it back to that team's coaches — no advertising, no profiling, no sharing outside the team. If you're a parent and want to know what's recorded about your child or want it removed, contact your child's coach directly, or email us at eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com and we'll help.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You control what you enter. You can edit or remove a player, a photo, or contact details at any time from the Roster tab.

You can delete your account at any time, entirely inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. Before you confirm, Squadly shows you exactly what will happen — because coaching often involves other people's teams, the effect depends on your role:

  • Teams you're the head coach of are permanently deleted, along with their rosters, games, and lineups. If other coaches are on that team, they lose access too — the app warns you about this before you confirm.
  • Teams you're an assistant coach on simply remove you as a member; the team and its data continue for the head coach and any other assistants.

Once you confirm, deletion happens immediately on our servers — your account and the data described above are erased, not just deactivated, and this cannot be undone. If you'd rather not delete your account but want a specific player's data removed, you can do that directly from the Roster tab without deleting anything else.

If you're ever unable to complete deletion in the app, email eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com and we'll take care of it manually.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know inside the app. Continuing to use Squadly after a change means you've had a chance to review it.

Questions, concerns, or a data request? Email eeshankhandelwal123@gmail.com — it goes straight to the developer, and we read everything.
